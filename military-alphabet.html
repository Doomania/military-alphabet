Usage Notes
Three rules for radio clarity:
1. Accuracy — use the exact codeword, not shortcuts.
2. Brevity — keep transmissions under 30 seconds.
3. Clarity — enunciate each syllable, pause between words.

Decimal point = "DECIMAL". Period = "STOP". Never say "repeat" on radio — say "SAY AGAIN" (repeat is reserved for artillery fire missions).

Call Sign Structure
Format: "[Recipient callsign], this is [Sender callsign], [message], Over."

Call signs identify units without revealing identities to interceptors. They rotate regularly. "Actual" appended to a callsign means the actual commander (not a subordinate). Famous example: "Maverick Actual" would mean the Maverick unit's actual commanding officer.

Critical Warnings
Never say "REPEAT" on radio. "Repeat" is an artillery command meaning "fire again." Always say SAY AGAIN when requesting retransmission.

Never say WILCO + ROGER together. WILCO already implies Roger. Using both is redundant and marks you as inexperienced.

Why These Specific Words?
The ICAO tested each codeword in noise, interference, and with speakers from 31 different languages. No two codewords may sound alike even at low signal quality. Spelling adjustments include: Alfa (not Alpha — prevents Spanish/Portuguese mispronunciation), Juliett (double-T prevents French speakers from silencing the final consonant), Oskar tested over "Oliver" and "Ontario" for better international clarity.
Use Outside the Military
Aviation
Squawk codes, ATIS updates, ATC coordination. Some airlines swap "Delta" to avoid airline name confusion.
Banking
Account verification, security codes, and large transaction confirmations over phone.
Emergency Services
Police, fire, and paramedics worldwide — combined with 10-codes for dispatch efficiency.
Cybersecurity
IR teams spell CVE IDs, IOC hashes, and IP addresses over voice comms during incidents.
Maritime
Coast Guard and shipping for vessel names, coordinates, and distress call identification.
Medicine
ER and ICU teams spell drug names and patient IDs to prevent fatal transcription errors.
