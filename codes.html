Critical Radio Rules
NEVER say "REPEAT" on radio. It is an artillery command meaning fire again. Always say SAY AGAIN.

NEVER say WILCO and ROGER together. WILCO already implies Roger.
NZ Police Notes
NZ Police use a modified 10-code set. Common NZ codes: 10-1 Signal weak · 10-4 Acknowledged · 10-20 Location · 10-76 En route.
