Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest way to memorize the NATO alphabet?

Short daily sessions beat one long cram: quiz random letters, spell real words aloud (names, number plates), and hammer the confusable pairs like Mike/November. Most people are fluent after a week of 10-minute drills.

What should I practice first — alphabet, Morse or 10-codes?

Start with the NATO alphabet: it is only 26 words, used everywhere from aviation to call centres, and every other radio skill builds on it. Add military time next, then Morse or 10-codes depending on your field.
